My Tuesdays have changed shape three times. Medical school in Colombia, a PhD lab at Harvard, consulting in New York. All three felt like a good life at the time, and they have almost nothing in common. I find that freeing rather than confusing. If the good life comes in that many shapes, there's no single right one to miss out on, which makes me want to aim higher, not play it safer.

I want to see more shapes. A hundred of them.
